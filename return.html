Return Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

We offer a return or exchange within the first 7 days from the date of your purchase. Please read this policy carefully before initiating a return or exchange request.

Return & Exchange Terms

schedule 7-Day Return Window

We offer a refund or exchange within the first 7 days from the date of your purchase. If 7 days have passed since your purchase, you will not be offered a return, exchange, or refund of any kind.

checklist Eligibility Conditions

To become eligible for a return or exchange, all of the following must apply:

  • The purchased item must be unused and in the same condition as you received it.
  • The item must have its original packaging.
  • Items purchased on sale may not be eligible for a return or exchange.

swap_horiz Exchange for Defective or Damaged Items

We replace items only when they are found to be defective or damaged. If you received a defective or damaged item, please contact our customer service team within the 7-day window to initiate an exchange.

category Exempt Categories

Certain categories of products or items are exempted from returns or refunds. Such exempt categories will be clearly identified to you at the time of purchase. Please review the product details carefully before completing your order.

inventory Inspection & Notification Process

Once your returned product is received and inspected by us, we will send you an email to notify you of the receipt of the returned item. If the return or exchange is approved following our quality check, your request will be processed in accordance with our policies.

Need Help?
