Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This refund and cancellation policy outlines how you can cancel or seek a refund for a product / service that you have purchased through the Platform. Under this policy:

Physical Products

Cancellations will only be considered if the request is made within 24 hours of placing the order, or prior to the dispatch of the item, whichever is earlier. Cancellation requests will not be entertained if the orders have been communicated to the sellers/merchants and they have initiated the process of shipping them.

Perishables & Religious Offerings: Mantralok Technologies LLP does not accept cancellation requests for perishable items like flowers, eatables, etc. Vendors are solely responsible for adhering to food safety and hygiene standards for Prasad and edible items. Refunds for perishables will only be considered if the user establishes the item was severely damaged or spoiled upon arrival.

In case of receipt of damaged or defective items, please report to our customer service team. The request would be entertained once the seller/ merchant listed on the Platform, has checked and determined the same at its own end. This should be reported within 7 days of receipt of products. The customer service team after looking into your complaint will take an appropriate decision.

In case of complaints regarding the products that come with a warranty from the manufacturers, please refer the issue to them.

Virtual Services (Online Pujas, Astrology, Consultations)

Cancellations: Bookings for virtual services (e.g., online pujas, consultations, astrology readings, etc.) can be cancelled for a full refund if the cancellation request is submitted at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled start time. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled start time are non-refundable.

Digital Goods: Purchases of downloadable digital content (e.g., recorded mantras, digital yantras, recorded consultation videos) are strictly non-refundable once the download or access link has been provided.

Vendor No-Shows: If a vendor or service provider fails to join a scheduled virtual service, users are entitled to a full refund or a rescheduled session, pending verification by our customer service team.

Religious Tourism & Pilgrimage (Yatra) Packages

Travel and pilgrimage packages involve advanced bookings with third-party transport and accommodation providers. The following standard cancellation policy applies unless a specific package dictates otherwise:

  • 30+ Days Prior to Departure: 100% refund, less a standard administrative processing fee.
  • 15 to 29 Days Prior to Departure: 50% refund of the total package cost.
  • Less than 15 Days Prior to Departure / No-Shows: 0% refund (strictly non-refundable).

Cancellations initiated by the Platform or travel operator due to extreme weather, safety concerns, or force majeure events will be eligible for a full refund or a free reschedule, at the platform's discretion.

Religious Event Management

Event Tickets: Tickets purchased for public religious events, seminars, or community gatherings are refundable up to 7 days prior to the event date. Tickets cancelled within 7 days of the event are non-refundable.

Custom Event Services: For users booking custom religious event management (e.g., private large-scale pujas or ceremonies), the initial booking deposit is strictly non-refundable to cover advance venue and priest blocking. The remaining balance may be refundable if the event is cancelled at least 14 days prior.

In case of any refunds approved by Mantralok Technologies LLP, it will take 5 to 7 business days for the refund to be processed to your original payment method.
