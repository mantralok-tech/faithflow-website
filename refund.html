Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy outlines how you can cancel or seek a refund for products/services purchased through the Mantralok platform.

Cancellation & Refund Terms

  • • Cancellation requests are considered only if made within 7 days of placing the order.
  • • Cancellation may not be entertained once the order has been communicated to sellers/merchants and the shipping process has started.
  • • For damaged or defective items, report the issue to customer support. Requests are processed after seller/merchant verification.
  • • For manufacturer-warranty products, complaints should be addressed directly to the manufacturer.
  • • If a refund is approved by Mantralok Technologies LLP, it will be processed within 5 days.

Need Help?

For support on cancellations/refunds, contact us at mantraloktech@gmail.com.
